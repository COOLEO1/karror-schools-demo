# Karror Schools — Multi-page Website Concept

A responsive multi-page school website concept prepared by Golden-Rhys Company.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `about.html` — Our story and values
- `academics.html` — Learning overview
- `campus.html` — Campus and school life
- `gallery.html` — Photo gallery
- `news.html` — News, notices and events
- `admissions.html` — Admissions information
- `contact.html` — Contact and WhatsApp enquiry

## Photography and permissions
The site uses illustrative Unsplash images, not photographs of Karror Schools. Image sources include:
- Doug Linstedt, classroom photo (Lagos, Nigeria): https://unsplash.com/photos/children-sitting-on-chairs-inside-classroom-jEEYZsaxbH4
- Oscar Omondi, teacher with learners (Mwingi, Kenya): https://unsplash.com/photos/a-woman-standing-in-front-of-a-group-of-children-Xz5kTUYAu9A

Both source pages label the photographs as free to use under the Unsplash License. Review the licence and source terms before production use. Do not use photographs copied from other private schools without written permission; student images also require appropriate parent/guardian consent. Best option: request Karror-approved photos of its own learners, teachers, campus and activities.

## Content accuracy
Public information reviewed for this concept indicates Karror Schools is based in Mangochi Boma and has been described as Montessori-based. All other class names, programmes, facilities, staff profiles, fees, term dates and admissions requirements must be confirmed by the school. The content is intentionally written as a concept and avoids fabricated school results or announcements.

The WhatsApp contact shown in the concept (+265 888 666 645) appeared in a public recruitment listing. Confirm it is still correct before launch.

## Functionality
The contact form prepares an enquiry in WhatsApp. It does not store messages or connect to a backend. Online applications, parent/student portals, payments, CMS/news publishing and secure file uploads require a separately scoped backend.
