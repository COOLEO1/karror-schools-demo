01 / THE KARROR STORY

A thoughtful start to lifelong learning

Every child has
a world of potential.

Karror Schools is a Montessori-based learning community in Mangochi, Malawi. Its approach places curiosity, independence and meaningful discovery at the heart of a child's early learning journey.

We believe education should help children build confidence, develop practical skills and feel excited about what they can learn next.
