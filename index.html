01 / WELCOME TO KARROR

A foundation for the future

Every child has
a world of potential.

Karror Schools is a Montessori-based learning community in Mangochi, Malawi. The school's learning philosophy places curiosity, independence and meaningful discovery at the heart of a child's journey.

We believe a positive school experience should nurture the whole child — supporting academic growth, confidence, creativity and a sense of belonging.
