WELCOME TO KARROR

Let's take the
first step.

Choosing a school is an important decision. We encourage families to contact Karror Schools directly to learn about current class availability, admission requirements and the next steps.

The steps below provide a simple guide for families. The school's official admissions process and requirements should be confirmed before launch.

01

Make an enquiry

Tell the school which class or age group you are enquiring about and ask any initial questions.

02

Learn about the school

Ask about the learning approach, available classes, school environment and any visit arrangements.

03

Confirm requirements

Get the official application details, documents required, fees and any important dates directly from the school.

04

Discuss enrolment

Follow the school's guidance on application submission and enrolment confirmation.
