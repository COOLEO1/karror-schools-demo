OUR STORY

Every learner deserves
a strong beginning.

Karror Schools is a Montessori-based learning community in Mangochi, Malawi. Its educational approach places curiosity, independence and meaningful discovery at the heart of a child's learning journey.

We believe education should support the whole child: building knowledge, confidence, practical skills and a positive relationship with learning.

This concept page is a starting point. The school's approved history, mission, vision and leadership profiles can be added once supplied by Karror.
